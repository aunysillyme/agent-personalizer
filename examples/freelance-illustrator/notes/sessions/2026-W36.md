## 2026-09-01 - print shop platform decision

**What happened:** compared hosting the print shop on a storefront service against building it.

- **decision:** hosted storefront until 100 units sold, then revisit. Why: a build is two weeks of drawing time for a shop with zero sales so far. (see decisions.md)
- **anti-pattern:** the AI proposed a full build plan before asking what the shop had sold. Ask the number first.
- **preference:** when comparing options, three columns: cost in drawing hours, cost in money, what it unlocks later.
