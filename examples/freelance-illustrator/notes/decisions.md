# Decisions log

*One running file. Every decision, newest at the bottom. Read before re-deciding anything. Each line names the date, the decision, the reason, and where it was decided.*

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · <decision> · why: <one line> · where: <session slug or file>`

A decision that reverses an earlier one names the earlier line and says "supersedes". Nothing is deleted.

---

- 2026-09-01 · print shop runs on a hosted storefront until 100 units sold · why: a self-built shop costs two weeks of drawing time for zero sales so far · where: print-shop-platform-decision
- 2026-09-03 · cover 7 keeps the cooler palette; warmth is for cover 8 only · why: the publisher's note named cover 8, not the series · where: cover-seven-colour-pass
